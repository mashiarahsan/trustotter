Four Pillars of My Approach

Retention

Catch disengagement early through activity monitoring and proactive outreach. Accounts don't go quiet without me knowing within a week. Save conversations are grounded in data, not discounts.

Expansion

Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities through usage patterns, client conversations, and timing around strong results. Growth happens naturally when clients see ROI first.

Client Experience

Every touchpoint builds trust. Proactive updates, fast responses, and making sure the client always knows where their account stands without having to ask.

Feedback Loop

Surface patterns across 300 accounts that no single client sees. Feed insights back to product, marketing, and sales so the entire business improves from what CS learns.

Growth Levers I'd Explore
Multiple paths to expansion revenue. The right lever depends on the client's stage and results.

  • Multi-Location Expansion

    Clients seeing results at one location are natural candidates for rolling out across additional locations.

  • Plan Tier Upgrades

    As usage grows and needs evolve, moving clients to higher-tier plans with additional features and support.

  • Longer Commitments

    When results are proven, converting month-to-month clients into quarterly or annual agreements.

  • Referral Generation

    Happy clients know other business owners. A well-timed ask after a strong result turns one account into two.

  • Case Study & Testimonial Harvesting

    Strong accounts become proof points. Their stories help sales close new business, and the client feels valued.

  • Feature Adoption Deepening

    Many clients use a fraction of what's available. Introducing underused features increases stickiness and value perception.

  • 💡 Annual Subscription at Discounted Rate

    When a client is happy and seeing results, offer a discounted yearly subscription. They lock in savings, we lock in 12 months of guaranteed revenue. Retention and expansion in one conversation — the client pays less per month, we eliminate monthly churn risk on that account entirely.

  • 💡 New Revenue Opportunity: Google Review Cards

    TrustOtter removes bad reviews. But what if we also helped businesses generate more positive ones faster? Physical Google review cards (NFC tap or QR code) that businesses hand to happy customers make leaving a review effortless. This is complementary to the core service: TrustOtter brings the bad reviews down, the cards bring the good reviews up. The result is a faster path to a higher Google rating for the client and a new recurring or per-order revenue stream for TrustOtter. Both sides win. See an example here →

Churn Signals I Watch For
Churn doesn't happen suddenly. It sends signals weeks before. My job is catching them.

Engagement Drop

Client stops logging in, stops replying, or stops checking results. Silence is the loudest churn signal.

Repeated Support Issues

Same problem surfacing multiple times means trust is eroding. Fix the root cause, not just the ticket.

Value Disconnect

Client doesn't connect what they're paying to what they're getting. This is a communication failure, not a product failure.

Renewal Window Silence

Client goes quiet as renewal approaches. They're evaluating. If I'm not proactively showing ROI, a competitor might be.

Operating Principles
1

Data Before Conversation

Every client touchpoint starts with pulling their numbers first. I never reach out without knowing where their account stands.

2

Proactive Over Reactive

If a client has to ask me for an update, I've already failed. They should hear from me before they think to reach out.

3

Save With Value, Not Discounts

When a client wants to cancel, I show them what they'd lose, not what I can take off the price. Discounts train clients to threaten cancellation.

4

Every Interaction Is Logged

Clean CRM records aren't a nice-to-have. They're how I keep 300 accounts from becoming 300 guesses.

5

Upsell Is Earned

I never pitch expansion to a client who isn't already seeing results. Timing matters more than the offer.
